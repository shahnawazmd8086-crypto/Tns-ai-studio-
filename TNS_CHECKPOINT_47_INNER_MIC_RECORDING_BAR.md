# Checkpoint 47 — Chat-box inner microphone recording bar

- Only the microphone inside the chat composer (`#homeChatDictation`) opens the screenshot-style recording bar.
- Recording bar includes cancel (X), animated waveform, stop (square), and send (blue arrow).
- Cancel aborts speech recognition and restores the text that existed before dictation.
- Stop ends recognition and keeps recognized text in the composer for review.
- Send submits recognized speech through the existing chat form.
- The microphone outside the chat box (`#homeChatVoice`) keeps its separate voice-conversation flow and styling.
- Browser speech recognition and microphone permission remain device/browser dependent.
