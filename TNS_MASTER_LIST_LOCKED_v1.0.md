# TNS Master List — Locked v1.0

**Status:** Locked planning reference  
**Brand:** TNS  
**Purpose:** Help people explain a problem, find reliable information, take supported next steps, and verify outcomes.  
**Important:** This is the target scope, not a claim that every feature is already implemented. Do not remove or change items without the user's approval.

## Main features

1. **TNS Problem Solver** — accept a problem through text, voice, photo, screenshot, or supported document; clarify the goal; propose next steps.
2. **Government Services** — explain schemes, eligibility, certificates, passport, pension, applications, official forms, status checks, and required documents.
3. **Complaint & Transparency** — find official complaint routes, prepare factual drafts, track reference details, explain appeals and escalation.
4. **Digital Work Assistant** — help with websites, forms, logins, uploads, common app errors, and online workflows.
5. **Documents & Files** — read supported files, summarize, explain, draft, convert, and organize documents where tools permit.
6. **Travel Assistant** — plan trips and compare travel options, including flights and transport where live data is available.
7. **Hotel & VIP Stay** — compare budget and premium hotels, location, amenities, total price, reviews, and cancellation terms.
8. **Shopping Assistant** — find products that match user needs and compare quality, features, price, and warranty.
9. **Best Deals** — compare available offers, return policies, seller signals, and total cost.
10. **Personal Services** — find doctors, clinics, restaurants, local services, appointments, and booking options.
11. **Budget Planner** — compare options against a user's budget and priorities.
12. **Research Assistant** — search reliable sources, prioritize official sources for official tasks, compare claims, and explain uncertainty.
13. **Language & Voice** — support simple-language interaction, Hindi/English, voice input, and additional languages as quality permits.
14. **Safe Digital Actions** — perform supported actions only when integrations exist and required user approval is given.
15. **Step-by-Step Guide** — guide the user when direct automation is unavailable.
16. **Case Management** — keep user-approved case summaries, tasks, dates, document references, and next steps.
17. **Result Verification** — distinguish advice, draft, submission, acknowledgement, approval/rejection, and actual resolution.
18. **Follow-up & Reminders** — remind users about deadlines, pending actions, appointments, and follow-ups where supported.
19. **Login & Account** — signup, login, recovery, session management, profile, and logout.
20. **Privacy & Security** — minimize data collection, protect files, obtain consent for sharing/actions, and provide deletion controls.
21. **Notifications** — show supported case updates and reminders without inventing statuses.
22. **Help & Support** — onboarding, feedback, error reports, and support routes where available.
23. **Simple Dashboard** — one chat-first home screen, with optional tools and saved work accessible without overwhelming the user.
24. **Review Existing TNS Studio ZIP** — audit and reuse suitable authentication, language, AI, storage, project, server, and creator foundations; do not delete old modules without approval.
25. **Testing & Quality** — test real functionality, security, error recovery, accessibility, and mobile usability before claiming completion.

## Five non-negotiable rules

1. **No false claims:** never say an action succeeded unless there is evidence.
2. **Outcome-first:** help the user progress toward the intended result, not just provide a generic answer.
3. **Permission first:** obtain clear approval before sensitive submissions, payments, bookings, or external data sharing.
4. **Reliable information:** prioritize official sources for government and other high-impact procedures; show uncertainty and source dates when available.
5. **Protect the user:** keep personal information and accounts safe.

## Locked home-dashboard direction

- Open directly to a clean, ChatGPT-style conversation.
- The user can immediately type, use supported voice input, or attach a supported photo/document.
- Keep the home screen uncluttered; expose creator and other tools through a collapsed “All tools” area.
- Support link/address/place-related requests in the conversation; live web/map results require suitable connected services.
- Keep existing modules available during the transition; do not remove them without approval.

## Delivery principle

The list is the target scope. Build in stages, verify each stage, disclose integrations that are missing, and do not treat a visual button as a working feature.
